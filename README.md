# Excel Business Analysis

A collection of business analysis projects completed in Microsoft Excel.

The projects demonstrate data analysis, pivot tables, business metrics, scenario modeling, and data visualization applied to practical business problems.

## Projects

### 1. Payment Operations Analysis

Analysis of payment processing data with a focus on transaction duration and operational anomalies.

The project includes:
- data exploration and aggregation;
- pivot table analysis;
- investigation of abnormal transaction duration;
- comparison by time, location, and server;
- data visualization and interpretation of results.

[View project](payment-operations-analysis/)

---

### 2. Online School Unit Economics

Analysis of the unit economics of an online education business and modeling of several business scenarios.

The project includes:
- CAC, Retention, Lifetime and LTR calculations;
- revenue and cost structure analysis;
- pivot tables and cross-sheet calculations;
- scenario analysis;
- evaluation of payroll changes;
- monetization of free lessons;
- analysis of the impact of Retention growth on LTR and profit margin.

[View project](online-school-unit-economics/)
